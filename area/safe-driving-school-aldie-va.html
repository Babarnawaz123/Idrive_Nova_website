Can I take the Driver Improvement Clinic online?

Yes. iDriveNova provides an online Driver Improvement Clinic course for Virginia drivers.

I failed my learner's permit test 3 times. What should I do?

You may be required to complete an 8-hour re-examination course before attempting the knowledge test again.

What payment methods are available?

After registration, payment can be made by Venmo, Zelle or credit card. Credit-card payments may carry an additional fee.
