04Online Course Guidance
Virginia Driver Education

Which online driving courses does iDriveNova offer?

Driver Improvement Clinic Online Course

The Driver Improvement Clinic is designed to enhance driving skills and driving-record knowledge. Participants learn traffic laws, courtroom procedures, defensive-driving strategies, hazard awareness, and safer decision-making. Completion may help with ticket dismissal or reduction, insurance savings, and certain license suspension or reinstatement situations.

Re-Examination Course: Three Failed Permit Tests

If you fail the knowledge exam three times, Virginia requires an approved classroom component before another attempt. The 8-hour course is based on the Virginia Driver's Manual and is designed for this requirement. Requirements differ by age: adults 18+ may complete the Driver's Manual course, while students under 18 must meet the applicable driver-education prerequisites before enrolling.
